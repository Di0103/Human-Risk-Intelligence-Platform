# ARMOUR
### Audit, Risk Mapping & Organizational Unified Remediation

**ARMOUR** is an enterprise-grade Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) platform delivering:
- **Zero-Trust Security & Adaptive OTP Authentication**: Simulated SMTP Relay TLS 1.3 dispatch with emergency auto-wipe session defense.
- **Continuous Compliance & Control Mapping**: Dynamic cross-walk across ISO/IEC 27001:2022, DPDP Act 2023, ISO 22301, COBIT 2019, and IT Act 2000.
- **Policy Management & Automated Live Scoring Engine**: Pre-configured enterprise policy templates evaluated across Completeness, Review Lifecycle, RACI Ownership, and Control Mappings.
- **Risk Register**: Likelihood vs. Impact 5x5 matrix scoring with risk treatment plans and threat intelligence exposure telemetry.
- **Audit Findings & Reports**: Formal regulatory attestation seal and print-ready compliance reports.
